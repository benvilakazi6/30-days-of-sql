### Your complete 15-query challenge

1. Display all customers.
2. Display only customer names.
3. Display products over R500.
4. Display orders from 2025.
5. Sort products from most expensive to cheapest.
6. Return the 10 most expensive products.
7. Find customers from Gauteng.
8. Find orders above R1,000.
9. Find customers whose names start with `A`.
10. Find products priced between R200 and R800.
11. Find customers from either Gauteng or KwaZulu-Natal.
12. Display orders between R500 and R2,000.
13. Display the 5 cheapest products.
14. Find products containing `Phone` in their name.
15. Count the total number of customers.

**Tip:** Try solving all 15 **without looking at the answers** first. This will give you good practice with `SELECT`, `WHERE`, `ORDER BY`, `LIMIT`, `AND`, `OR`, `LIKE`, `BETWEEN`, and `COUNT`.
